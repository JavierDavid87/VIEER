HOW TO PUBLISH SO ANYONE CAN VIEW YOUR PORTFOLIO

Keep index.html, style.css and script.js together in one folder.

1. EDIT (only you): double-click index.html. Upload your quizzes, exams,
   activities and projects, add your photo, and click your name, bio and
   details to edit them. Everything is saved in your browser.

2. EXPORT: click "Export site". A file named portfolio-content.zip downloads.
   Unzip it into the SAME folder as index.html. You will now see
   content.json and an uploads folder next to your three code files.

3. PUBLISH: upload the whole folder to a free host, for example
   Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) or GitHub Pages. You get a link
   you can send to anyone.

Visitors see your profile and all your files, view-only. They cannot upload
or delete anything. To see the editing mode on your live site, add ?admin
to the end of the link.

TO UPDATE LATER: open the LOCAL index.html again, make changes, export
again, replace content.json and the uploads folder, and upload again.
Any file type works (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, PDF, pictures).
